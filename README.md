# Plant Disease & Leaf Chlorosis Diagnosis Matrix

A symptom-based diagnostic troubleshooting tree for urban and backyard vegetable gardeners experiencing foliage discoloration, spore spots, and pest damage.

## Diagnostic Fast Reference

### 1. Solanum Lycopersicum (Tomato)
* **Lower Leaves Yellowing Uniformly**: Natural senescence or nitrogen mobility drawdown.
* **Yellow Leaves with Concentric Dark Rings**: Early Blight (*Alternaria linariae*).
* **Detailed Diagnostic Breakdown**: [Tomato Leaves Turning Yellow Troubleshooting Guide](https://theblogdaily.com/tomato-leaves-turning-yellow/)

### 2. Cucumis Sativus (Cucumber)
* **Yellowing Between Green Leaf Veins**: Interveinal chlorosis (Magnesium/Iron deficiency).
* **Mottled Yellowing with White Spores**: Powdery or Downy Mildew.
* **Full Fix Protocol**: [Cucumber Leaves Turning Yellow Remedies](https://theblogdaily.com/cucumber-leaves-turning-yellow/)

### 3. Capsicum Annuum (Peppers)
* **Drooping Yellow Leaves with Wet Soil**: Root asphyxiation from over-saturation.
* **Nitrogen Deficiency Recovery**: [Pepper Plant Yellow Leaves Fix Guide](https://theblogdaily.com/pepper-plant-yellow-leaves/)

### 4. Aphid & Soft-Bodied Pest Remediation
* **Remediation Protocol**: [How to Get Rid of Aphids Naturally](https://theblogdaily.com/how-to-get-rid-of-aphids-naturally/)
